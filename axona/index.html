Why now

Three shifts are moving the script.

Sources: Capsa leadership briefing, Aug 2026; central fill planning analysis (estimates)

Reimbursement is compressing

Margin moves from the spread to the fill. 340B reimbursement cuts, MFN pricing, Medicare benchmarks and PBM reform compress the spread.

The med mix is changing

~52%

of new medicines projected to be non-oral, 2026 to 2035. Pens, cartons and cold chain, not counted pills.

Care is moving beyond the hospital

Growth moves beyond the hospital. GLP-1s and biologics flow through clinics, mail and direct to patient. Pharma-direct pricing opens a path around the PBM.
